<h2 style="color: #c09d5c; border-bottom: 2px solid #4a3f35; padding-bottom: 5px;">ABOUT:</h2>
<p style="color: #c0c0c0;"><b>OdinArchitect</b> is the ultimate construction expansion for Valheim. Whether you are building a cozy mountain cabin, a sprawling village, or a legendary fortress, this mod gives you the intricate tools to bring your wildest designs to life. <i>If you can dream it, you can build it!</i></p>

<p style="color: #d4af37;"><b>IMPORTANT UPDATE NOTICE:</b> Before updating to version 1.4.0 or newer, please <b>delete your old config file</b> and any existing OdinArchitect localization files to prevent conflicts!</p>

<h2 style="color: #c09d5c; border-bottom: 2px solid #4a3f35; padding-bottom: 5px;">FEATURES:</h2>

<ul style="color: #dcdcdc; list-style-type: square;">
    <li><b style="color: #a08050;">Custom Building Tools:</b>
        <ul>
            <li><b>Odin's Hammer:</b> Crafted at a Workbench (Wood + Stone). The key to your new build menu.</li>
            <li><b>Stone Chisel:</b> Crafted at a Stone Cutter (Wood + Iron). Essential for Dvergr-themed marble mastery.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li><b style="color: #a08050;">Mechanical Marvels:</b>
        <ul>
            <li>🔥 <b>Small & Big Elevators:</b> Elevate your base design (literally!).</li>
            <li>🔥 <b>Functional Drawbridges:</b> Complete with floating Surtling Lanterns for that epic aesthetic.</li>
            <li><b>Hidden Hatches:</b> Keep your secret loot safe and out of sight.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li><b style="color: #a08050;">Automated Smelters:</b>
        <ul>
            <li><b>Advanced Cooking Station:</b> Perfect for high-volume feasts.</li>
            <li>🔥 <b>Compost:</b> Turn raw meat into worms.</li>
            <li>🔥 <b>Bird House & Fish Trap:</b> Use your worms to passively harvest feathers and raw fish!</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li><b style="color: #a08050;">Architectural Variety:</b> Huge selection of gates, including Iron/DarkWood variants without central poles, and Crystal-windowed doors.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #c09d5c; border-bottom: 2px solid #4a3f35; padding-bottom: 5px;">INSTALLATION:</h2>
<p style="color: #c0c0c0;"><b>Both Server and Client must have this mod installed.</b></p>
<ol style="color: #c0c0c0;">
    <li>Ensure you have the latest <b>BepInEx</b> and <b>Jotunn (JVL)</b> installed.</li>
    <li>Place the <b>OdinArchitect</b> folder directly into: <code>Bepinex/plugins/</code></li>
    <li><b>AzuAnticheat Notice:</b> Make sure to include <code>OALanternFix.dll</code> in your plugin folders and whitelists/greylists.</li>
</ol>
